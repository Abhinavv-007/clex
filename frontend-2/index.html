Smart Routing

Three Paths.
One Decision Engine.

Clex detects your network, finds the other device, and picks the fastest route — or lets you choose.

Primary

Direct P2P

Browser-to-browser transfer via WebRTC. Files go straight from you to them. No server in between.

Protocol WebRTC
Server Storage None
Account Not Required
Privacy Maximum
Speed Boost

Local Network

Same Wi-Fi? Same network? Clex detects it and transfers at local speed. Blazing fast for large files.

Protocol Local TCP
Speed LAN Speed
Requirement Same Network
Ideal For Large Files
Fallback

Google Drive

When direct isn't possible, Clex uses Google Drive as a secure relay. Encrypted, temporary, under your control.

Protocol Google APIs
Encryption Google TLS
Account Google Auth
Storage Your Drive
