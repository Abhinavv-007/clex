Open the workspace
Step by step

Six steps, start to finish

  1. 1
    Open the workspace

    It is the first thing on clex.in. It opens instantly in your browser, with no account.

    Tip: bookmark clex.in and it is always one click away.

  2. 2
    Drop your files

    Drag files in from your desktop, click the drop zone to browse, or paste an image. PDFs, images, documents, archives, anything.

    Tip: paste images straight from the clipboard with Ctrl V or Cmd V.

  3. 3
    Prepare, if you like

    Tools appear for the files you added: compress images, merge PDFs, convert Word to PDF, bundle a ZIP. Everything happens in the browser.

    Tip: when a tool finishes, Clex suggests the next one or takes you to sharing.

  4. 4
    Share

    Clex shows a six character code, a link and a QR. Send any of them. The other person opens it in their browser and the transfer starts.

    Tip: both tabs need to stay open until the transfer is done.

  5. 5
    Watch it arrive

    A live progress bar, chunk rail and health score show the transfer. Pause any time and resume from the next chunk. When it lands, both sides get a verified receipt.

    Tip: a health score above 90 is a clean run. Lower usually means retries kicked in, which is the safety net working.

  6. 6
    Receiving instead

    Got a code? Open clex.in/receive, type it in, and the file lands in your browser, checked chunk by chunk.
