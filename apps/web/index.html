How it moves

One tab, three moves

No hopping between a converter, a compressor and a file host. The file you drop is the file you prepare is the file you send

  1. 01

    Drop

    Drag files in, paste from the clipboard or pick them from your device. They stay in this tab's memory and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    • Images
    • PDF

    • Anything else
  2. 02

    Compress a photo, convert its format, merge or split PDFs, turn a Word file into a PDF and bundle it all into a ZIP. Every tool runs in the browser.

    • Merge

Clex Direct+

Every chunk accounted for

Direct transfers are cut into 64 KB chunks and acknowledged one by one. Lost chunks are resent, damaged ones are re-requested by hash, and a transfer can pause and pick up where it stopped. When the last chunk lands, both sides get a receipt

  • Chunk manifestsize and SHA-256 for every chunk
  • ACK and retryonly missing chunks are resent
  • Pause and resumeno restart from byte zero
  • Health scorea live reading from 0 to 100
  • Verified receiptcontent free, with a root hash
  • Compatibleolder peers fall back on their own
Delivery

Two routes, both direct

Whichever you pick, the file goes from one browser straight to the other. There is no relay in the middle holding a copy

Can't connect either way? Clex tells you instead of quietly uploading. Keep both tabs open until the transfer is done.

Vault, inside the workspace

The private side built in

Flip the workspace to Vault for encrypted notes and secret links. Notes are encrypted before they touch your device's storage, sync only between devices you pair, and never appear on the public chain

  • Encrypted notes and folders, searchable and offline
  • Peer to peer sync between paired devices
  • Secret links with expiry, view once and copy guards
  • QR hand-offs and short reveal codes
